Own you.eth? Claim you.tocsin.eth.

One rule: whoever owns label.eth on ENSv2 Sepolia can claim label.tocsin.eth. One transaction. Nobody approves it, and nobody can take it back, which you can verify below before you spend gas.

01 Connect

Sepolia only. The page switches your wallet if needed. You pay gas; the name costs 8 test USDC, which the page mints for you.

02 Check a label

.eth → .tocsin.eth

03 Register the .eth name if you do not own it

Mint test USDC, approve, commit, wait 60 seconds, register. Five transactions. Skip this if you already own the name.

04 Claim

Deploys your own Permissioned Resolver if you have none, calls claim on the MirrorRegistrar, sets the addr record. Up to three transactions.

05 Verify the guarantees, live

Read straight from the Sepolia contracts. "Emancipated" means no account holds a role that can change or remove any name in the registry.
