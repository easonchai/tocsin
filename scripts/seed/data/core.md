# core: seed list

This is the first list published to Tocsin, the append-only on-chain log of scam infrastructure described in the spec (`docs/spec/threat-flag-log.md`). It will be published under `core.tocsin.eth`. It covers four families of attacker infrastructure seen on EVM chains between late 2022 and November 2026: wallet-drainer kits (Inferno, Angel, Pink, Monkey), address-poisoning crews (the May 2024 $68M WBTC case, the Dec 2025 $50M USDT case, the Jan 2026 4,556 ETH case, the Aug 2026 USDT case), EIP-7702 sweeper delegates (the CrimeEnjoyor family) and the proceeds wallets of protocol and exchange hacks (Bybit, WazirX, Radiant, Balancer V2 and 30 others from DeFiHackLabs and rekt.news).

Every entry carries one evidence transaction hash. Six independent finders proposed candidates from published research (Check Point Research, SlowMist, Bitquery, CertiK, QuillAudits, nullteilerfrei, rekt.news, DeFiHackLabs) and each candidate was then re-verified by replaying the transaction against a public RPC or Blockscout: receipt status, sender, recipient, Transfer/Approval logs, and where needed a callTracer trace for internal ETH transfers or an `eth_getCode` check for `0xef0100` delegation designators. Entries whose verification failed are listed under Rejected. Entries that passed the transaction check but whose subject type or evidence type does not match the spec category definition are also held out of the seed JSON (listed under Rejected, grouped separately) so they can be promoted later; an append-only log cannot take them back. The list names only attacker infrastructure: operator wallets, spender and factory contracts, cashout and affiliate receivers, lookalikes that were actually paid, counterfeit tokens, batch-poisoning senders and funders, sweeper delegates and their collectors. No victim address is a subject. Victim addresses appear only, truncated, inside notes.

Totals: 92 accepted (seed JSON), 2 failed verification, 38 held for spec fit. Chains: Ethereum (85), BSC (4), Arbitrum (4, incl. one address also on BSC), Optimism (1), Base (1), Linea (1).
